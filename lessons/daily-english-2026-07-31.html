Cyber-Attackers Take 607,000 Records from Department for Education

Today’s Friday society and education lesson uses a BBC News report on a cyber incident affecting the UK Department for Education and public-service data systems.

Level: intermediateTime: 5–10 minutes
Source article

Title: Cyber-attackers take 607,000 records from Department for Education

Source: BBC News

Published: July 29, 2026

Original article: BBC News article link

Article Summary

BBC News reports that cyber-attackers took 607,000 records from systems connected to the UK Department for Education. The department said the stolen information included telephone numbers and email addresses for individuals and organisations, but not bank details or other highly sensitive information.

The DfE said it contained the incident quickly and is working with the National Cyber Security Centre and the National Crime Agency. The report also notes that cyber incidents in education are becoming more common, with many schools and further-education institutions reporting regular breaches or attacks.

文章摘要

BBC News 報導，網路攻擊者從英國教育部相關系統取得 60.7 萬筆紀錄。教育部表示，被取得的資料包括個人與機構的電話號碼和電子郵件地址，但不包括銀行資料或其他高度敏感資訊。

教育部表示，事件已迅速受到控制，並正與國家網路安全中心及國家犯罪局合作處理。報導也指出，教育領域的網路事件日益頻繁，許多學校與進修教育機構都回報曾遭遇定期的資料外洩或攻擊。

5 Daily Words

records

pieces of stored information, especially official data kept by an organisation.

In article: “Cyber-attackers take 607,000 records from Department for Education”

紀錄、資料；由機構保存的正式資訊或資料項目。

incident

an event, especially one that causes concern, needs investigation, or disrupts normal activity.

In article: “The DfE confirmed the incident and said it is working closely with the National Cyber Security Centre and the National Crime Agency (NCA).”

事件；特別指引發關切、需要調查或影響正常運作的事情。

contained

stopped from spreading or becoming worse; kept under control.

In article: “The data protection risk to individuals is not considered high and it is understood the attack was contained quickly.”

受控制的、被遏止的；沒有繼續擴大或惡化。

breach

a failure to protect something, especially when private data or a secure system is accessed without permission.

In article: “Around a quarter of further education institutions (24%) reported experiencing a breach or attack at least weekly according to the government's most recent Cyber Security Breaches Survey.”

違規、破口、資料外洩；尤指私人資料或安全系統遭未授權存取。

circumstances

the facts, conditions, and details surrounding an event, often used in formal investigations.

In article: “An NCA spokesperson said: ‘We are aware of an incident affecting the Department for Education and are working with partners to understand the circumstances and impact.’”

情況、背景條件；圍繞某事件的事實、條件與細節，常用於正式調查語境。
