Review Launched into How Pub and Hotel Business Rates Are Calculated

Today’s Monday business lesson uses a BBC Business report on a UK Treasury review of business rates for pubs and hotels, a useful topic for tax, property, and small-business vocabulary.

Level: intermediateTime: 5–10 minutes
Source article

Title: Review launched into how pub and hotel business rates calculated

Source: BBC Business

Published: August 24, 2026

Original article: BBC article link

Article Summary

BBC Business reports that the UK Treasury has launched a review into how business rates are calculated for pubs and hotels in England and Wales. The review will examine rate valuations and gather views from landlords, hoteliers, and business owners before reporting back in March 2027.

The article explains that pub groups say they face disproportionately high rates bills, while other business groups want wider reform. The debate connects local hospitality businesses, property valuations, tax relief, small-business thresholds, and the next rates revaluation in 2029.

文章摘要

BBC Business 報導，英國財政部已啟動一項審查，檢視英格蘭與威爾斯的酒吧和飯店商業稅率如何計算。這項審查將研究稅率估值，並徵詢房東、旅館業者與企業主的意見，預計在 2027 年 3 月提出報告。

文章指出，酒吧團體認為他們承擔的稅率帳單高得不成比例；其他企業團體則要求更全面的制度改革。這場討論牽涉地方餐旅業、房產估值、稅務減免、小企業門檻，以及 2029 年下一次稅率重新估值。

5 Daily Words

review

an official examination of a policy, rule, system, or decision to decide whether it should change.

In article: “A review is being launched into the way business rates are calculated for pubs and hotels in England and Wales, and could lead to a reform of the system.”

審查、檢討；由官方或組織仔細檢視政策、規則或制度，以判斷是否需要改變。

valuations

official estimates of how much a property, business, asset, or company is worth.

In article: “The Treasury says business rates expert Jerry Schurder will lead the review into rate valuations and report back in March 2027...”

估值；對房產、企業、資產或公司的價值所作的正式估算。

disproportionately

in a way that is too large or too small compared with something else, especially in an unfair or unbalanced way.

In article: “Pub groups have argued they face disproportionately higher rates bills, but other businesses have called for a wider reform of the rates system.”

不成比例地；相較於其他事物過大或過小，常帶有不公平或失衡的意思。

threshold

a limit or level at which a rule starts to apply, a payment becomes due, or support becomes available.

In article: “However, Beaumont said the government needed to address the wider business rates system and exempt more smaller firms by increasing the rates relief threshold for small businesses.”

門檻、起算點；規則開始適用、款項需要支付，或補助資格開始生效的界線。

revaluation

a formal process of calculating the value of something again, often for tax, accounting, property, or currency purposes.

In article: “Schurder is a former business rates policy lead at advisory firm Newmark UK, and his review will feed into the next rates revaluation in 2029.”

重新估值；為了稅務、會計、房產或貨幣等目的，再次正式計算某物價值的程序。
