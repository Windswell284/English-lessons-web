The Race to Stop England Running Out of Water

Today’s Sunday lighter public-interest lesson uses a BBC Science & Environment feature about drought, household demand, leaks, reservoirs, and the choices England faces as water supplies come under pressure.

Level: intermediateTime: 5–10 minutes
Source article

Title: The race to stop England running out of water

Source: BBC News

Published: August 29, 2026

Original article: BBC article link

Article Summary

BBC News reports that parts of England are facing serious water pressure after a very dry summer, with farms, rivers, households, and businesses all competing for limited supplies. The article explains that climate change, rising demand, high consumption, leakage, and delayed reservoir construction could leave England with a major daily water shortfall by 2055.

The report looks at possible solutions, including reducing household use, changing business incentives, detecting leaks with fibre optic technology, building new reservoirs, and recharging underground aquifers. It also shows why the issue is not only technical: society must decide how to balance water for homes, food production, nature, and industry.

文章摘要

BBC News 報導，英格蘭部分地區在乾旱夏季後面臨嚴重供水壓力，農場、河流、家庭與企業都在爭取有限水源。文章指出，氣候變遷、需求上升、高用水量、漏水問題以及水庫建設延宕，可能使英格蘭到 2055 年出現重大的每日供水缺口。

報導也介紹可能的解方，包括降低家庭用水、調整企業用水誘因、用光纖技術偵測漏水、興建新水庫，以及補充地下含水層。不過，問題不只是技術層面：社會還必須決定如何在家庭、糧食生產、自然環境與產業之間分配水資源。

5 Daily Words

drought

a long period with little or no rain, often causing water shortages and damage to crops or ecosystems.

In article: “Image caption, Daniel's farm has fared poorly in the drought.”

乾旱；指長時間幾乎沒有降雨，常造成缺水、農作物受損或生態系壓力。

irrigation

the controlled supply of water to land or crops, especially when rainfall is not enough.

In article: “River levels are low but have recovered enough to allow one night of irrigation tonight.”

灌溉；指有計畫地把水供應給土地或農作物，尤其是在雨量不足時使用。

shortfall

the amount by which something is less than what is needed, expected, or promised.

In article: “The result could be that by 2055 England will face a shortfall of five billion litres in public water supply a day.”

缺口、短缺量；指實際數量低於所需、預期或承諾數量的差額。

potable

safe to drink; a formal technical word often used in public-health, utility, and infrastructure reporting.

In article: “Potable water is water that is free of harmful bacteria and chemicals and is considered safe to drink and cook with.”

可飲用的；較正式、技術性的字，常見於公共衛生、公用事業與基礎設施報導。

abstraction

the formal process of taking water from a natural source such as a river, lake, or underground store; common in environmental regulation.

In article: “As well as Daniel Sizer's Suffolk farm, the EA has now suspended or limited 1,327 farm licences for abstraction - taking water from rivers.”

取水、抽取水資源；指從河流、湖泊或地下水等自然來源取水的正式程序，常見於環境管制語境。
