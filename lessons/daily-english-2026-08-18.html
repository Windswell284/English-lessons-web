Trump Threatens Oman Over Iran Deal

Today’s Tuesday world-affairs lesson uses a BBC News report on tensions around Oman, Iran, and the Strait of Hormuz, with vocabulary for diplomacy, security, and negotiation.

Level: intermediateTime: 5–10 minutes
Source article

Title: Trump threatens to bomb US ally Oman if it 'gets in the way' over Iran deal

Source: BBC News

Published: August 17, 2026

Original article: BBC News article link

Article Summary

BBC News reports that President Donald Trump threatened Oman, a US ally, as Oman and Iran pursued separate talks about reopening the Strait of Hormuz. The waterway is economically important because a large share of global oil and liquefied natural gas usually passes through it.

The article says US-Iran talks face uncertainty as a 60-day memorandum of understanding approaches expiry. Oman presents itself as neutral in the conflict, while Washington is pursuing its own deal and Iran is discussing transit routes, compensation, and conditions for reopening the strait.

文章摘要

BBC News 報導，美國總統川普在阿曼與伊朗就重新開放荷莫茲海峽進行談判之際，威脅這個美國盟友。荷莫茲海峽對全球經濟非常重要，因為全球相當大比例的石油與液化天然氣通常會經由此處運輸。

文章指出，美國與伊朗的談判前景不明，因為雙方一份為期 60 天的諒解備忘錄即將到期。阿曼宣稱自己在衝突中保持中立；同時，華府尋求自己的協議，伊朗則討論航行路線、補償與重新開放海峽的條件。

5 Daily Words

threat

a warning that someone may do something harmful, especially to pressure another person, group, or country.

In article: “A reporter for Fox said the US president made the threat to Oman while using an expletive during a phone interview.”

威脅；表示可能採取傷害性行動的警告，常用於政治、外交或安全新聞。

expiring

coming to an end because an official time limit, agreement, or period is finished.

In article: “Meanwhile, the future of talks between the US and Iran is unclear as a 60-day Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the two sides is expiring.”

即將到期、期滿；指協議、期限、證件或安排的有效期間快要結束。

negotiating

discussing an issue formally in order to reach an agreement or settle a dispute.

In article: “However it has suffered a number of strikes from Iran and is now playing a key role in negotiating with Tehran to reopen the waterway.”

談判、協商；為了達成協議或解決爭端而進行正式討論。

compensation

money or another form of repayment given because of loss, damage, injury, or a broken agreement.

In article: “Earlier this month, Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said a full reopening was dependent on Iran receiving compensation from the US for what Tehran described as violations of the MoU.”

補償、賠償；因損失、損害、受傷或協議受違反而提供的金錢或其他補償。

constitute

to be considered as, amount to, or legally count as a particular thing or action.

In article: “It is not clear what Trump thinks would constitute getting in the way of his ambitions for a deal.”

構成、被視為；常用於法律、政策與正式分析，表示某事可算作某種行為或情況。
