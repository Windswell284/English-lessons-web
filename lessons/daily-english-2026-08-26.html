World's Oceans Hit Highest Temperature on Record as El Niño Grows

Today’s Wednesday science-and-climate lesson uses a BBC News report on record ocean heat, El Niño, and why warmer seas can affect weather, sea levels, and marine life.

Level: intermediateTime: 5–10 minutes
Source article

Title: World's oceans hit highest temperature on record as El Niño grows

Source: BBC News

Published: August 24, 2026

Original article: BBC article link

Article Summary

BBC News reports that the world’s oceans have reached their highest recorded average surface temperature outside the polar regions. Scientists say the timing is striking because sea temperatures usually peak around March or April, while the current El Niño has not yet reached its expected peak.

The article explains that warmer oceans can worsen extreme weather, raise sea levels, and put pressure on marine habitats such as coral reefs. Researchers link the longer-term trend to human-driven climate change, while El Niño is adding more heat to an already stressed ocean system.

文章摘要

BBC News 報導，極地以外的全球海洋平均表面溫度創下有紀錄以來新高。科學家指出，這個時間點特別值得注意，因為海溫通常在三月或四月達到年度高峰，而目前的聖嬰現象尚未達到預期最強階段。

文章說明，海洋升溫可能加劇極端天氣、推升海平面，並對珊瑚礁等海洋棲地造成壓力。研究人員認為，長期升溫趨勢與人為氣候變遷有關，而聖嬰現象則是在已經承受壓力的海洋系統上再增加熱量。

5 Daily Words

warmer

having a higher temperature; in climate reporting, often used to describe rising heat in air, water, or land.

In article: “Warmer oceans can have wide-reaching consequences, including supercharging extreme weather, raising sea levels and harming marine life.”

更溫暖的、溫度更高的；在氣候報導中，常用來描述空氣、水域或陸地熱度上升。

consequences

results or effects that happen because of an action, event, or condition, especially important or serious results.

In article: “Warmer oceans can have wide-reaching consequences, including supercharging extreme weather, raising sea levels and harming marine life.”

後果、影響；由某個行動、事件或狀況造成的結果，尤其指重要或嚴重的結果。

phenomenon

an observable event, pattern, or fact, especially one that is unusual, important, or studied scientifically.

In article: “The world's oceans are hotter than ever recorded, new data suggests, as they suffer from human-caused climate change and the growing El Niño weather phenomenon.”

現象；可觀察到的事件、模式或事實，尤指不尋常、重要，或受到科學研究的現象。

intensify

to become stronger, more serious, or more extreme; or to make something stronger, more serious, or more extreme.

In article: “They can provide storms with extra moisture and energy, and can intensify heatwaves on land in some coastal regions by reducing the cooling effect of sea breezes.”

加強、加劇；變得更強烈、更嚴重或更極端，或使某事變得更強烈、更嚴重或更極端。

exerts

applies or brings to bear a force, pressure, or influence; often used in formal science, policy, and analysis writing.

In article: “The southern hemisphere contains more of the planet's ocean surface than the northern hemisphere and so exerts a bigger influence on average sea temperatures.”

施加、發揮（力量或影響）；常見於正式的科學、政策或分析寫作，表示某因素對結果產生作用。
