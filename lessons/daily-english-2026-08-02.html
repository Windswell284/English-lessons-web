Where to See the Biggest Solar Eclipse Since 1999

Today’s Sunday lighter lesson uses a BBC News skywatching feature about the UK’s unusually dramatic partial solar eclipse on 12 August.

Level: intermediateTime: 5–10 minutes
Source article

Title: Where to see the biggest solar eclipse since 1999

Source: BBC News

Published: July 31, 2026

Original article: BBC News article link

Article Summary

BBC News reports that people in parts of the UK’s West Country may get their best view of a solar eclipse in nearly three decades on 12 August, when as much as 95% of the sun could disappear behind the moon. Cornwall, Devon, Somerset and Bristol are among the places expected to have especially strong viewing conditions if the weather is clear.

The article explains that the UK will not see a total eclipse, but the event will still be unusually dramatic because the moon will cover most of the sun. Astronomers advise viewers to choose elevated places with a clear western horizon and to use certified eclipse glasses or other safe viewing methods.

文章摘要

BBC News 報導，英國西南部部分地區的人們可能在 8 月 12 日看到近三十年來最佳的日食景象，屆時最多約 95% 的太陽會被月亮遮住。若天氣晴朗，康沃爾、德文、薩默塞特和布里斯托等地預計會有特別好的觀賞條件。

報導說明，英國這次不會看到日全食，但由於月亮將遮住大部分太陽，這仍會是相當壯觀的天文事件。天文學家建議觀賞者選擇地勢較高、向西視野開闊的地點，並使用經認證的日食眼鏡或其他安全觀測方式。

5 Daily Words

view

what you can see from a particular place, especially when watching an event or looking across an area.

In article: “People across the West Country will get their best view of a solar eclipse in nearly three decades when up to 95% of the sun disappears behind the moon on 12 August.”

景觀、視野；從某個位置能看到的景象，尤其指觀賞事件或眺望某地時的視線範圍。

obscured

covered or hidden so that something cannot be seen clearly.

In article: “People in Bristol, Somerset, Gloucestershire and Wiltshire are expected to see around 90% of the Sun obscured, while Cornwall and the Isles of Scilly should get some of the best views anywhere in the UK with 95%.”

被遮住的、被遮蔽的；某物被覆蓋或隱藏，因此無法清楚看見。

celestial

relating to the sky, space, stars, planets, the sun, or the moon.

In article: “The rare celestial event will be the most dramatic eclipse visible from the UK since 1999, with Cornwall, Devon, Somerset and Bristol among the best places in the UK to watch it.”

天體的、天空的；與天空、太空、星星、行星、太陽或月亮有關。

unobstructed

not blocked by anything, so a person can see, move, or act freely.

In article: “Astronomers recommend elevated locations with unobstructed views to the west.”

無阻礙的、視野開闊的；沒有被任何東西擋住，因此能自由觀看、移動或行動。

totality

the state of being complete; in astronomy, the path or moment when the sun is completely covered during a total eclipse.

In article: “They said although the ‘arc of totality – the path the eclipse shadow takes - misses us here in the UK, we will still get to see over 90% of the sun covered by the moon.’”

全食、完全狀態；在天文語境中，指日全食時太陽被完全遮住的路徑或時刻。
