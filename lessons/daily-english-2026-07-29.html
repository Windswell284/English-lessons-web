Mysterious Elephant Deaths in Kenya Prompt Urgent Inquiry

Today’s Wednesday science and environment lesson uses a BBC News report on the unexplained deaths of 14 elephants near Kenya’s Amboseli National Park.

Level: intermediateTime: 5–10 minutes
Source article

Title: Mysterious deaths of 14 elephants in Kenya prompts urgent inquiry

Source: BBC News

Published: July 28, 2026

Original article: BBC News article link

Article Summary

BBC News reports that Kenyan authorities have opened an urgent investigation after 14 elephants were found dead in southern Kenya near Amboseli National Park. Wildlife experts are examining the bodies and sending tissue samples to laboratories to identify whether disease, poisoning, or another factor caused the deaths.

The report says the deaths are unusual because they involve elephants of different ages and sexes, and because the region has not recorded elephant deaths on this scale for decades. Officials say there is currently no evidence pointing to a single cause.

文章摘要

BBC News 報導，肯亞南部安博塞利國家公園附近有 14 頭大象死亡，當局已展開緊急調查。野生動物專家正在檢查屍體，並把組織樣本送往實驗室，以判定死因是否為疾病、中毒或其他因素。

報導指出，這起事件特別不尋常，因為死亡的大象年齡和性別不同，而且該地區數十年來沒有記錄到如此規模的大象死亡。官員表示，目前還沒有證據指向單一死因。

5 Daily Words

urgent

needing quick action or attention because the situation is important.

In article: “An urgent investigation has been launched into the mysterious deaths of 14 elephants in southern Kenya near the Amboseli National Park.”

緊急的、急迫的；因情況重要而需要快速處理或關注。

carcasses

dead bodies of animals, especially large wild animals.

In article: “The Kenya Wildlife Service (KWS) told the BBC that experts were examining the carcasses and said that tissue samples had been sent to laboratories...”

動物屍體；尤其指大型野生動物的遺體。

ecosystem

a connected system of living things, land, water, climate, and other natural features in a place.

In article: “The park and its surrounds are known as the Amboseli ecosystem, a vast area that straddles the border with Tanzania...”

生態系；某地的生物、土地、水、氣候等自然條件彼此連結形成的系統。

demographic

a group within a population, often described by age, sex, location, income, or other shared characteristics.

In article: “The carcasses include elephants of different ages and sexes, suggesting the deaths are not limited to a single family or demographic.”

人口群體、族群分類；依年齡、性別、地區、收入或其他共同特徵劃分的群體。

mortalities

deaths within a population; a formal scientific or public-health term for cases of death.

In article: “Officials say this is the first time in decades that elephant mortalities on this scale have been recorded in the region...”

死亡數、死亡事件；科學或公共衛生語境中，用來指某個族群中的死亡案例。
