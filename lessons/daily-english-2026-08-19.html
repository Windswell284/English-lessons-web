How Soon Will the Drought Be Over?

Today’s Wednesday science and climate lesson uses a BBC News explainer on UK drought recovery, with vocabulary for water resources, climate risk, and environmental planning.

Level: intermediateTime: 5–10 minutes
Source article

Title: How soon will the drought be over?

Source: BBC News

Published: August 18, 2026

Original article: BBC News article link

Article Summary

BBC News explains that recent rain in parts of the UK does not automatically end a drought. Scientists say the key question is whether water resources have recovered, because rivers, reservoirs, soil stores, and underground rock layers may need months of sustained rainfall to refill.

The article also describes how heat, low rainfall, and high demand can create fast-moving drought conditions. Experts warn that climate change may bring sharper swings between dry weather and sudden flooding, making water supplies, farming, and infrastructure harder to manage.

文章摘要

BBC News 解釋，英國部分地區近期下雨，並不代表乾旱已經結束。科學家指出，真正重要的是水資源是否恢復，因為河流、水庫、土壤中的水分，以及地下岩層中的儲水，都可能需要好幾個月的穩定降雨才能補回來。

文章也說明，高溫、降雨不足與用水需求增加，可能讓乾旱快速形成。專家警告，氣候變遷可能使乾旱與突發洪水之間的轉換更加劇烈，讓供水、農業與基礎建設管理變得更困難。

5 Daily Words

drought

a long period with too little rain, causing water shortages or very dry land.

In article: “Contrary to public perception, a drought does not end with a few days of rain.”

乾旱；長時間降雨不足，導致缺水、土地乾燥或水資源壓力增加。

reservoirs

large natural or artificial places where water is collected and stored for public use.

In article: “Water must flow back into rivers, reservoirs, and the stores beneath our feet in the tiny spaces between soil, sand, and rock.”

水庫、蓄水處；用來收集與儲存供水的大型天然或人工水體。

depleted

greatly reduced in amount, strength, or supply because too much has been used up.

In article: “Soil is like a type of reservoir - those stores are depleted by thirsty trees and plants, and evaporation during hot temperatures.”

耗盡的、大量減少的；指資源、能量或儲量因消耗而變少。

aquifers

underground layers of rock, sand, or soil that hold water and can supply wells or springs.

In article: “Hydrologists should know by February or March whether aquifers and human-made reservoirs have been replenished.”

含水層；地下可儲存並供應水的岩層、沙層或土層。

volatility

a state of changing quickly and unpredictably, often creating risk or instability.

In article: “Researchers warn this growing ‘volatility’ could make water supplies, agriculture and infrastructure harder to manage as conditions switch rapidly between extremes.”

波動性、不穩定性；指情況快速且難以預測地變化，常帶來風險或管理困難。
