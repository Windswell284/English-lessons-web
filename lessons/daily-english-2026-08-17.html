New Rules Make Pub Conversions Harder

Today’s Monday business lesson uses a BBC News article on new planning rules that make it harder to turn pubs into homes or offices, connecting local business survival with property development and regulation.

Level: intermediateTime: 5–10 minutes
Source article

Title: Changing pubs into homes to be made harder under new rules

Source: BBC News

Published: August 16, 2026

Original article: BBC News article link

Article Summary

BBC News reports that England’s updated National Planning Policy Framework is expected to give pubs more protection from being converted into homes or offices. Under the new rules, developers will have to show evidence that a pub has no reasonable prospect of continuing as a business.

The article explains the business tension behind the policy: pub and hospitality groups welcome protection for community assets, but say tax, employment costs, VAT, and business rates remain major reasons pubs are closing. The planning update also includes changes meant to speed up housebuilding near railway stations.

文章摘要

BBC News 報導，英格蘭更新後的《國家規劃政策框架》預計會加強保護酒吧，讓開發商更難把酒吧改建成住宅或辦公室。根據新規定，開發商必須提出證據，證明該酒吧沒有合理前景可以繼續經營。

文章也說明這項政策背後的商業矛盾：酒吧與餐飲業團體歡迎保護社區資產，但他們也指出，稅負、雇用成本、增值稅與商業地價稅仍是酒吧倒閉的重要原因。這次規劃更新也包含加快火車站周邊住宅建設的措施。

5 Daily Words

closure

the act or result of a business, service, or place shutting down permanently or for a long time.

In article: “The updated National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF) will come into force on Monday and is expected to contain more protections for pubs at threat of closure for conversion to another use.”

關閉、停業；常用來指商店、酒吧、公司或公共服務停止營運。

consultation

a formal process of asking people or organisations for opinions before a decision is made.

In article: “A previous consultation on the plans only gave the protection to the last pub in the area.”

諮詢、徵詢意見；政府或機構在制定政策前，向相關人士或團體蒐集意見的正式程序。

conversion

the process of changing something from one form, purpose, or use into another.

In article: “The Campaign for Real Ale has previously said that pubs are being ‘lost forever to conversion or demolition as developers look to cash in on the desirable locations and unique architecture of pubs and social clubs’.”

轉換、改建；指把某物從一種用途或形式改成另一種，例如把酒吧改成住宅。

statutory

required, created, or controlled by law rather than by custom or informal agreement.

In article: “The Gardens Trust, a parks and gardens heritage and conversion charity, called the decision to remove its role as a statutory consultee ‘devastating’.”

法定的；由法律規定、建立或要求的，常見於政策、政府與法律新聞。

consultee

a person or organisation that must be asked for advice or comments before an official decision is made.

In article: “The Gardens Trust, a parks and gardens heritage and conversion charity, called the decision to remove its role as a statutory consultee ‘devastating’.”

受諮詢者、被徵詢意見的機構或人士；在正式決策前，需要被要求提供意見的一方。
