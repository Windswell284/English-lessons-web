Does the West Still Have Influence in Afghanistan?

Today’s Saturday world-affairs lesson uses a BBC News analysis on Afghanistan, Taliban rule, international recognition, sanctions, and the limits of Western leverage five years after the Taliban returned to power.

Level: intermediateTime: 5–10 minutes
Source article

Title: Five years after the Taliban swept to power, does the West have any influence?

Source: BBC News

Published: August 14, 2026

Original article: BBC News article link

Article Summary

BBC News examines whether Western governments still have meaningful influence over Afghanistan five years after the Taliban took power. The article describes severe restrictions on women and girls, humanitarian pressure, and the difficult question of how other countries should engage with Taliban authorities.

The report says international responses remain divided. Some Western governments still focus on rights, sanctions, and formal recognition, while other countries have expanded practical ties through diplomacy, trade, and embassies. Analysts quoted by the BBC suggest that traditional leverage has weakened, leaving Afghanistan in a tense and uncertain diplomatic balance.

文章摘要

BBC News 分析塔利班重新掌權五年後，西方國家對阿富汗是否仍有實質影響力。文章描述女性與女孩面臨的嚴格限制、人道危機壓力，以及各國應如何與塔利班當局接觸的困難問題。

報導指出，國際社會的做法仍然分歧：一些西方政府強調人權、制裁與正式承認；其他國家則透過外交、貿易和使館往來擴大實務關係。BBC 引述的分析認為，傳統的施壓籌碼已經變弱，使阿富汗處在緊張而不穩定的外交平衡中。

5 Daily Words

banned

officially not allowed; prohibited by a rule, law, authority, or organisation.

In article: “Five years after this ultra-conservative Islamist movement took charge, women are banned from much of the public sphere and girls are prohibited from going to school over the age of 12.”

被禁止的；指某件事因法律、規定或當局命令而不能做。

divisive

likely to cause strong disagreement or split people into opposing sides.

In article: “The issue is deeply divisive.”

造成分裂或強烈爭議的；常用於政治、社會或政策議題。

edicts

official orders or commands issued by a person or authority with power.

In article: “These include women's rights, unpredictable Taliban edicts, international sanctions and aid cuts during a spiralling humanitarian crisis.”

法令、命令；通常指由掌權者或官方機構發布、帶有權威性的指示。

sanctions

official penalties, often economic or diplomatic, used by countries to pressure another government or group.

In article: “But they're still strangled by international sanctions linked to terrorism and human rights.”

制裁；國家或國際組織為施壓而採取的經濟、外交或法律限制。

equilibrium

a state of balance between different forces, pressures, or interests; often formal in politics, economics, and science.

In article: “An unstable and potentially dangerous equilibrium, is how the situation was described in closed-door remarks by a senior UN official at a recent international gathering.”

平衡狀態、均衡；在政治或經濟語境中，指多方力量暫時維持的脆弱平衡。
