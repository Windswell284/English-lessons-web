The Change That May Help First-Time Buyers Get a Mortgage

Today’s Monday business lesson uses a BBC News article about looser UK mortgage rules, larger loans, and the financial risks facing first-time home buyers.

Level: intermediateTime: 5–10 minutes
Source article

Title: The change that may help you get a mortgage as a first-time buyer

Source: BBC News

Published: August 2, 2026

Original article: BBC News article link

Article Summary

BBC News reports that rule changes and more flexible lending in the UK may allow some first-time buyers to borrow up to six or even seven times their annual income. That could bring home ownership within reach for people who have struggled with high house prices, rising mortgage rates, and the difficulty of saving for a deposit.

The article also warns that bigger loans carry risk. After the 2008 financial crisis, mortgage regulation became stricter because reckless lending was blamed for serious damage to banks and households. Buyers may still need strong credit histories, steady salaries, enough savings, and a financial buffer in case interest rates, jobs, or personal circumstances change.

文章摘要

BBC News 報導，英國房貸規則放寬、貸款條件更有彈性後，部分首次購屋者可能可以借到年收入六倍、甚至七倍的房貸。對於因房價高、房貸利率上升、存頭期款困難而難以買房的人來說，這可能讓購屋變得更接近現實。

但文章也提醒，借更多錢同時代表更高風險。2008 年金融危機後，英國加強房貸監管，因為不負責任的放款被認為重創銀行與家庭。購屋者仍可能需要良好的信用紀錄、穩定薪資、足夠存款，以及在利率、工作或個人狀況改變時能應對的財務緩衝。

5 Daily Words

deposit

an amount of money paid at the beginning of a purchase, especially the savings used toward buying a home.

In article: “It hard to save for a deposit when the cost of living is so high, the average house price is nearly £300,000 and interest rates on new mortgages are rising.”

頭期款、訂金；購買房屋等大型商品時一開始支付的一筆錢，通常來自買方的存款。

lending

the act of giving money to someone with the expectation that it will be paid back, usually with interest.

In article: “Reckless mortgage lending was blamed for the financial crisis of 2008, which brought some banks to their knees and saw people lose their homes.”

放款、借貸；把錢借給他人並期待對方日後償還，通常還要支付利息。

regulation

official rules or controls that tell companies, markets, or people what they are allowed to do.

In article: “Regulation limited how much lenders were able to lend - technically, only 15% of their new mortgages could be at higher than 4.5 times loan-to-income.”

監管、法規；政府或主管機關制定的正式規則，用來限制或管理公司、市場或個人的行為。

criteria

standards or conditions used to decide whether someone or something qualifies for a choice, loan, job, or approval.

In article: “There is still a strict criteria you most likely need to meet as a first-time buyer to be offered a larger mortgage.”

標準、條件；用來判斷某人或某事是否符合資格、能否獲得貸款、工作或批准的要求。

markedly

in a way that is clear, noticeable, and significant; not just slightly.

In article: “The greater flexibility could mean that first time buyers that felt ownership was still out of reach may find that the amount they can borrow has changed markedly in a relatively short time.”

顯著地、明顯地；變化清楚可見且幅度重要，不只是些微改變。
