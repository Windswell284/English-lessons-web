How to See July’s Full Buck Moon

Today’s Sunday lighter lesson uses a BBC Weather skywatching feature about July’s full Buck Moon, with vocabulary for astronomy, seasons, and forecasts.

Level: intermediateTime: 5–10 minutes
Source article

Title: How to see July's full Buck Moon as it rises in the night sky

Source: BBC Weather

Published: July 26, 2026

Original article: BBC Weather article link

Article Summary

BBC Weather explains that July’s full Moon, known as the Buck Moon, will be visible across much of the UK in the coming week. It reaches peak fullness on 29 July, although the exact peak will happen while the Moon is below the UK horizon.

The article connects the name to the season when male deer grow new antlers, and it also previews other summer sky events, including the Perseid meteor shower, a total solar eclipse visible in parts of Europe, and a partial lunar eclipse later in August.

文章摘要

BBC Weather 說明，七月滿月又稱為「雄鹿月」，下週將在英國多地夜空中可見。滿月會在 7 月 29 日達到最圓的時刻，但在英國，這個精確時間點月亮仍位於地平線下方。

文章也解釋，「雄鹿月」這個名稱和雄鹿在此季節長出新鹿角有關，並預告夏季其他天象，包括英仙座流星雨、歐洲部分地區可見的日全食，以及八月底的月偏食。

5 Daily Words

overnight

during the night; in weather and news reports, from evening through the following morning.

In article: “However, skygazers won't miss out; it will still appear as-full to the naked eye overnight on Tuesday and Wednesday.”

在夜間、整夜；在天氣或新聞報導中，常指從晚上到隔天早晨的期間。

horizon

the line where the sky seems to meet the land or sea; also used figuratively for the limit of what can be seen or expected.

In article: “The exact moment of its maximum fullness will be missed across the UK as the Moon will be just below the horizon.”

地平線；天空看似與陸地或海面相接的線，也可比喻視野或預期的界限。

antlers

the branched horns that grow on the heads of animals such as deer, usually shed and regrown each year.

In article: “The July full Moon gets its name from the time of year when male deer, or bucks, rapidly grow a new set of antlers.”

鹿角；鹿等動物頭上分枝狀的角，通常會定期脫落並重新長出。

celestial

relating to the sky, space, stars, planets, or other objects beyond Earth.

In article: “What else is happening in the summer sky? The Buck Moon is just one of a number of celestial sights to look for this summer.”

天體的、天空的；與天空、太空、星星、行星或地球以外的物體有關。

totality

the state of being complete; in astronomy, the short phase of a total eclipse when the Sun or Moon is fully covered.

In article: “The 12 August eclipse will be the first total solar eclipse visible over mainland Europe since 1999, with Spain and northern Portugal being the only places to see totality.”

全食階段；在天文學中，指日食或月食中天體被完全遮住的短暫完整階段，也可指完整狀態。
