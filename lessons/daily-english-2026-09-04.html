Emergency Measures Urged as Teachers Consider Quitting

Today’s Friday society and education lesson uses a BBC News report on teacher workload, burnout, and proposals to improve retention in England’s schools.

Level: intermediateTime: 5–10 minutes
Source article

Title: Emergency measures needed to stop teachers quitting, say Lib Dems

Source: BBC News

Published: August 31, 2026

Original article: BBC article link

Article Summary

BBC News reports that the Liberal Democrats are calling for emergency measures to stop teachers leaving the profession in England. A party survey found that nearly half of teachers questioned were considering quitting within five years, with many saying they felt overworked, stressed, and frequently burnt out.

The proposals include fully funding teacher pay rises, enforcing legally mandated working hours, limiting overtime, and requiring schools to introduce flexible working policies. The Department for Education said teacher-leaving rates were recently among the lowest since 2010, but acknowledged that more support is still needed.

文章摘要

BBC News 報導，英國自由民主黨呼籲採取緊急措施，防止英格蘭教師離開教職。該黨一項調查指出，受訪教師中將近一半正在考慮於未來五年內離開教室，許多人表示自己工作過量、壓力大，並且經常感到精疲力竭。

相關提案包括由政府全額支付教師加薪、執行法律規定的工作時數、限制加班，並要求所有學校制定彈性工作政策。英國教育部表示，去年教師離職率是 2010 年以來較低的水準之一，但也承認仍有更多工作要做。
