How Heatwaves Could Reshape the School Calendar

Today’s Sunday feature lesson uses a BBC Science & Environment report on how hotter summers may force schools to rethink term dates, exams, classroom ventilation, and family holiday habits.

Level: intermediateTime: 5–10 minutes
Source article

Title: How a new era of heatwaves could shake up the school calendar

Source: BBC Science & Environment

Published: August 22, 2026

Original article: BBC article link

Article Summary

BBC Science & Environment reports that repeated heatwaves are prompting new questions about whether the UK school calendar still fits a warming climate. Many classrooms lack air conditioning, and extreme heat has already led some schools to close early or send pupils home.

The article explains that hotter weather is arriving earlier in the year, overlapping with exams and normal class time. Experts, parents, and educators are now debating whether school holidays, building design, ventilation, and heat-warning rules need to adapt as summers become longer, hotter, and more disruptive.

文章摘要

BBC Science & Environment 報導，接連出現的熱浪讓人重新思考英國學校行事曆是否仍適合暖化中的氣候。許多教室沒有冷氣，極端高溫已使部分學校提早關閉或讓學生提前回家。

文章指出，炎熱天氣正更早到來，並與考試和正常上課時間重疊。專家、家長與教育工作者正在討論：暑假安排、校舍設計、通風設備與高溫警示規則，是否都需要隨著更長、更熱、更容易干擾教學的夏季而調整。

5 Daily Words

heatwaves

periods of unusually hot weather that last for several days and may affect health, work, travel, or school routines.

In article: “Multiple temperature records have been broken with five widespread heatwaves and four consecutive months where the temperature has exceeded 35C.”

熱浪；持續數日的異常高溫天氣，可能影響健康、工作、交通或上學安排。

ventilation

the movement of fresh air through a room or building, especially to keep it safe, cool, or comfortable.

In article: “With no air conditioning and limited ventilation, the heat became stifling and more than 1,000 schools across the UK either closed or sent pupils home early...”

通風；新鮮空氣在房間或建築內流動，以維持安全、涼爽或舒適。

stifling

so hot, airless, or oppressive that it becomes hard to breathe, concentrate, or feel comfortable.

In article: “With no air conditioning and limited ventilation, the heat became stifling and more than 1,000 schools across the UK either closed or sent pupils home early...”

悶熱得令人窒息的；形容空氣不流通、炎熱壓迫，使人難以呼吸、專心或感到舒適。

impair

to weaken or damage someone’s ability to do something well.

In article: “Baroness Brown, chair of the UK's Climate Change Adaptation committee, has warned that ‘we know high temperatures can significantly impair concentration and performance’ of children in schools.”

削弱、損害；使某人做某事的能力變差，常用於注意力、判斷力或表現。

prevalence

the fact of being common or widespread within a place, period, or group.

In article: “Will Lang, chief meteorologist at the Met Office, says the ‘de facto hot season’ in the UK is lengthening, with increasing prevalence of hot weather...”

盛行、普遍程度；某種情況在某地、某時期或某群體中常見或廣泛存在的程度。
