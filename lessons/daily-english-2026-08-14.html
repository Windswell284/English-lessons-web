Are the UK's Top Universities Really “Hoovering Up” Students?

Today’s Friday society and education lesson uses a BBC News report on UK university admissions, selective institutions, and the pressure on universities to recruit students after results day.

Level: intermediateTime: 5–10 minutes
Source article

Title: Are the UK's top universities really 'hoovering up' students?

Source: BBC News

Published: August 13, 2026

Original article: BBC News article link

Article Summary

BBC News reports that a record number of UK students secured university places in 2026, with the biggest increase going to the most selective, higher-tariff universities. Some analysts say top institutions are accepting more strong applicants, even when those students narrowly miss their original grade offers.

The article also explains why universities may want to grow: many are under financial pressure because of frozen domestic tuition fees and fewer international students. University leaders reject the idea that standards are being lowered, arguing that admissions decisions still depend on evidence that students can succeed.

文章摘要

BBC News 報導，2026 年英國有創紀錄數量的學生取得大學名額，而成長最明顯的是最具選擇性的「高門檻」大學。有分析人士認為，頂尖大學正在錄取更多優秀學生，即使有些學生沒有完全達到原本的成績條件。

文章也說明，大學想擴大招生有現實壓力：本地學費長期凍結、國際學生減少，讓許多學校財務吃緊。大學領袖則否認降低標準，強調招生仍會看學生是否有能力在課程中成功。

5 Daily Words

accepted

allowed to join, enter, or take part in a school, university, job, programme, or group.

In article: “The number of students accepted into higher-tariff universities in 2026 was 5.5% higher than last year.”

被錄取、被接受；在教育或工作語境中，常指申請人獲准進入學校、課程或機構。

selective

choosing only some people or things from a larger group, usually because standards are high.

In article: “But the biggest increase in acceptances was for the most selective, ‘higher-tariff’ universities – such as those in the Russell Group.”

有選擇性的、錄取門檻高的；常用來形容學校、公司或制度只挑選部分申請者。

institutions

large organisations with an important public role, such as universities, banks, courts, or government bodies.

In article: “Nick Hillman, chief executive of the Higher Education Policy Institute (Hepi), said the top institutions were ‘hoovering up all the crumbs’ when it came to the top students.”

機構、制度性組織；通常指大學、銀行、法院、政府機關等具有公共角色的組織。

redundancies

job losses that happen because an employer no longer needs particular roles or cannot afford to keep them.

In article: “He said universities taking on more students meant less popular courses, such as modern languages, could be kept running, keeping staff redundancies at bay.”

裁員、職位裁撤；多指雇主因財務、需求或組織調整而取消工作職位。

utilising

using something effectively for a practical purpose, especially resources, space, time, or capacity.

In article: “While there had been a ‘significant drop in international students’ - who are largely postgraduates, and who pay higher fees - Hillman said universities were utilising their resources for UK undergraduates instead.”

有效利用、運用；比 using 更正式，常用於資源、能力、設備或時間的配置。
