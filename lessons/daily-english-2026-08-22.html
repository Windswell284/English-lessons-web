Israel Re-establishes West Bank Settlement Despite Protests

Today’s Saturday world-affairs lesson uses a BBC News report on Israel’s reopening of Kadim, a West Bank settlement closed two decades ago, and the wider international-law and local-security concerns around settlement expansion.

Level: intermediateTime: 5–10 minutes
Source article

Title: Israel re-establishes closed West Bank settlement, defying growing international protests

Source: BBC News

Published: August 21, 2026

Original article: BBC News article link

Article Summary

BBC News reports that Israeli settlers have returned to Kadim in the occupied West Bank, about twenty years after a previous Israeli government evacuated and closed the settlement. The move comes after Israel’s current government overturned a ban on Jewish settlements around Jenin.

The article says nearby Palestinian residents fear the rapid expansion of settlements, new outposts, and military construction. It also notes that Israeli settlements and settler outposts are illegal under international law, while Israeli officials and settler leaders describe the return as a political and historical “correction.”

文章摘要

BBC News 報導，以色列定居者重返約旦河西岸被佔領地區的 Kadim；這個定居點約二十年前曾由以色列前政府撤離並關閉。這項行動發生在以色列現政府推翻 Jenin 周邊猶太定居禁令之後。

文章指出，附近巴勒斯坦居民擔心定居點、新前哨站與軍事建設快速擴張。報導也說，以色列定居點與定居者前哨站依國際法屬非法；但以色列官員與定居者領袖則把重返 Kadim 描述為政治與歷史上的「修正」。

5 Daily Words

settlement

a place where people establish a community, often in a politically disputed or newly occupied area.

In article: “A new generation of Israeli settlers has returned to the settlement of Kadim, in the occupied West Bank, two decades after it was closed by a previous Israeli government.”

定居點；人們建立社群居住的地方，常用於政治爭議或被佔領地區的脈絡。

expansion

the act of becoming larger, broader, or more extensive, especially in territory, policy, or operations.

In article: “Palestinian residents are watching the new push on settlement expansion here with fear.”

擴張；範圍、領土、政策或行動規模變大的過程。

outposts

small, separate bases or settlements set up away from a main community or centre of authority.

In article: “All Israeli settlements and settler outposts are illegal under international law.”

前哨站、外圍據點；設在主要社群或權力中心之外的小型基地或定居點。

unilateral

done by one side or party without the agreement or equal participation of others.

In article: “Kadim, on a small hillside outside the Palestinian city of Jenin, was evacuated in 2005 by a previous Israeli government as part of a wider unilateral Disengagement Plan...”

單方面的；由一方自行決定或執行，而未取得其他各方同等參與或同意。

orchestrated

carefully planned and coordinated, often in a way that suggests deliberate control behind events.

In article: “Now, we live in the hell orchestrated by them and their assaults,” he said.

精心策劃並協調的；常暗示事件背後有刻意安排與控制。
