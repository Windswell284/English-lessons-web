Ten Killed in Russian Attack on Drone Exhibition Near Kyiv

Today’s Saturday world-affairs lesson uses a BBC report on Russian missile strikes near Kyiv, with vocabulary for military reporting, security, and diplomacy.

Level: intermediateTime: 5–10 minutes
Source article

Title: Ten killed in Russian attack on drone exhibition near Kyiv

Source: BBC News

Published: July 25, 2026

Original article: BBC News article link

Article Summary

BBC News reports that Russian attacks on Ukraine killed at least 15 people, including 10 people in ballistic missile strikes on an exhibition near Kyiv that was showcasing drone technology. The event was attended by prominent members of Ukraine’s defence sector, and President Volodymyr Zelensky said about 100 people were injured.

The article says Russia confirmed the attack and claimed the site was connected to drones used against Russian targets. Ukrainian officials are investigating why the public event was held and what safety measures were in place, while both sides reported strikes on military or logistics-related targets.

文章摘要

BBC News 報導，俄羅斯對烏克蘭的攻擊造成至少 15 人死亡，其中包括在基輔附近一場展示無人機技術的展覽遭彈道飛彈攻擊後死亡的 10 人。這場活動有烏克蘭國防產業的重要人士出席，澤倫斯基總統表示約有 100 人受傷。

文章指出，俄羅斯證實發動攻擊，並聲稱該地點與用於攻擊俄羅斯目標的無人機有關。烏克蘭官員正在調查為何舉辦這類公開活動，以及現場採取了哪些安全措施；同時，雙方都通報了針對軍事或後勤相關目標的攻擊。

5 Daily Words

attacks

violent actions against a person, place, or country; in news reports, often military strikes or assaults.

In article: “At least 15 people have been killed in Russian attacks on Ukraine, including 10 dead in ballistic missile strikes on an exhibition near Kyiv showcasing drone technology.”

攻擊；指對人、地點或國家採取的暴力行動，在新聞中常指軍事打擊或襲擊。

prominent

well known, important, or easy to notice in a particular field or situation.

In article: “Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said some 100 people had been injured in the daytime attack on the exhibition, attended by prominent members of the defence sector.”

重要的、知名的、顯著的；在某個領域或情況中具有影響力或容易被注意到的。

ballistic

relating to missiles or objects that are launched and then travel through the air under the force of gravity.

In article: “Ukrainian air force spokesman Yuri Ihnat told Ukrainian TV the attack involved three ballistic missiles, only one of which was intercepted.”

彈道的；指飛彈或物體發射後依彈道飛行，常用於描述彈道飛彈。

intercepted

stopped, caught, or blocked before reaching the intended destination or target.

In article: “Ukrainian air force spokesman Yuri Ihnat told Ukrainian TV the attack involved three ballistic missiles, only one of which was intercepted.”

攔截、截住；在抵達目的地或目標之前被阻止、擋下或捕獲。

weaponry

weapons considered as a group, especially the equipment used by a military force.

In article: “Russia confirmed the attack. Its Defence Ministry said high-precision long-range weaponry had been used on a site where drones for use in targeting civil sites in Russia were being demonstrated.”

武器裝備；作為整體來看的武器，特別是軍隊使用的軍事設備。
