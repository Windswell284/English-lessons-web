One in Eight Young People Still Out of Work or Education Ahead of Major Review

Today’s Friday society and work lesson uses a BBC News report on young people in the UK who are not in education, employment, or training, and the policy review expected to recommend new support.

Level: intermediateTime: 5–10 minutes
Source article

Title: One in eight young people still out of work or education ahead of major review

Source: BBC News

Published: August 27, 2026

Original article: BBC article link

Article Summary

BBC News reports that UK statistics show about 981,000 people aged 16 to 24 were not in education, employment, or training between April and June. That is roughly one in eight young people, and the figure remains higher than the same period last year.

The article says former minister Alan Milburn is expected to publish recommendations for tackling the problem, while government ministers point to job guarantees, work-experience placements, apprenticeships, and hiring incentives. The report also highlights long-term causes such as health issues, disadvantage, and the weakening of traditional routes into work.

文章摘要

BBC News 報導，英國最新統計顯示，今年四月至六月約有 98.1 萬名 16 至 24 歲年輕人未就學、未就業、也未接受培訓，約等於每八名年輕人就有一人。這個數字雖比今年前三個月下降，但仍高於去年同期。

文章指出，前部長 Alan Milburn 即將提出處理此問題的建議；政府則提到就業保證、工作體驗名額、學徒制與聘僱補助等措施。報告也強調，健康問題、社會弱勢，以及傳統就業途徑逐漸削弱，都是造成長期挑戰的因素。

5 Daily Words

employment

paid work, or the state of having a job; a common word in economic, education, and social-policy news.

In article: “Just under one million young people in the UK are not in education, employment or training (Neet).”

就業、受僱；指有薪工作或擁有工作的狀態，常見於經濟、教育與社會政策新聞。

recommendations

official suggestions about what should be done, often made after a review, report, or investigation.

In article: “The final instalment of a major report by former minister Alan Milburn is expected to set out recommendations for tackling the problem in the coming weeks.”

建議、建議事項；常指審查、報告或調查後提出的正式處理方案。

robust

strong and able to continue performing well; in policy and business news, often used to describe systems, markets, or evidence.

In article: “Work and Pensions Secretary Pat McFadden said the UK labour market was ‘in a robust position’ but there had been ‘a long-term challenge for young people’.”

強健的、穩固的；在政策或商業新聞中，常用來形容制度、市場或證據具有韌性、表現穩定。

incentives

rewards or benefits designed to encourage people or organizations to take a particular action.

In article: “McFadden said there were also plans for 300,000 more work experience placements and 50,000 more youth apprenticeship starts, adding that the government had already rolled out £3,000 hiring incentives for employers who take on young people who have been out of work for six months.”

誘因、激勵措施；指用獎勵或好處鼓勵個人或組織採取某種行動。

erosion

the gradual weakening, loss, or wearing away of something; a formal abstract noun often used for trust, rights, standards, or social pathways.

In article: “In May, the first part of Milburn’s report, which was commissioned by the government, said the reasons for the rise in rates among men were ‘complex’, but could be down to more health issues, the ‘erosion of traditional employment routes’ and ‘a growing gap in educational outcomes’.”

逐漸削弱、侵蝕；正式抽象名詞，常用於信任、權利、標準或社會途徑逐步流失的情況。
