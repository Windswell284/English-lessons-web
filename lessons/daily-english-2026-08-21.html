GCSE Resit Cycle Is “Simply Not Working,” Education Leaders Say

Today’s Friday society and education lesson uses a BBC News report on England’s GCSE resit system and a planned new qualification for students who do not pass maths or English.

Level: intermediateTime: 5–10 minutes
Source article

Title: ‘Relentless’ GCSE resit cycle ‘simply not working’, union says

Source: BBC News

Published: August 20, 2026

Original article: BBC News article link

Article Summary

BBC News reports that school and college leaders in England say the repeated GCSE resit cycle for maths and English is “simply not working.” Many older students retake exams they previously failed, but pass rates remain much lower than for 16-year-olds taking the exams for the first time.

The government says it wants to end an “endless treadmill” of unnecessary resits by introducing a new Level 1 “stepping stone” qualification from 2028. Supporters hope the change will give students a more realistic route forward, though education leaders say the details are still limited and the wider causes of low attainment need attention much earlier.

文章摘要

BBC News 報導，英格蘭的學校與學院領袖表示，數學與英文 GCSE 不斷重考的循環「根本行不通」。許多年紀較大的學生重考先前未通過的考試，但通過率仍遠低於第一次應考的 16 歲學生。

英國政府表示，希望透過 2028 年開始推出新的 Level 1「踏腳石」資格，終止不必要重考的「無止盡跑步機」。支持者希望這能給學生更實際的前進路徑，但教育界也指出，目前細節仍有限，而且低成就的深層原因需要更早處理。

5 Daily Words

resitting

taking an exam again after not passing it, or after trying to improve an earlier result.

In article: “Thursday’s results data showed nearly one in four maths and English GCSEs in England were taken by students aged 17 and older, mostly young people resitting exams they had previously failed.”

重考；在未通過考試或想提高成績後，再次參加同一類考試。

proportion

a share or part of a whole amount, group, or total.

In article: “The Association of School and College Leaders (ASCL) said a ‘significant proportion’ of students fall short of passing those subjects...”

比例、部分；某個整體數量或群體中的一部分。

treadmill

a repetitive situation that takes effort but seems to make little real progress.

In article: “The government said it wanted to stop the ‘endless treadmill of unnecessary resits’.”

（比喻）無止盡的重複循環；花很多力氣卻似乎沒有真正進展的狀況。

qualification

an official record, certificate, or level showing that someone has completed training, study, or an assessment.

In article: “It said it would introduce a new ‘stepping stone’ qualification from 2028 at Level 1...”

資格、證書；正式顯示某人完成訓練、學習或評量的紀錄或等級。

demoralising

making people lose confidence, hope, or motivation, especially after repeated difficulty.

In article: “She said this year’s resit figures were ‘disappointing and slightly demoralising for everybody’ but not a ‘huge surprise’.”

令人沮喪、打擊士氣的；特別是在反覆受挫後，使人失去信心、希望或動力。
