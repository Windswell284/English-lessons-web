The US-Canada Trade War in 5 Charts

Today’s Monday business lesson uses a BBC News article about tariffs, counter-tariffs, exposed industries, and how the trade fight is reshaping business decisions on both sides of the US-Canada border.

Level: intermediateTime: 5–10 minutes

Title: The US-Canada trade war in 5 charts

Source: BBC News

Published: August 31, 2026

Original article: BBC article link

Article Summary

BBC News reports that the US and Canada remain locked in a trade dispute, with the US placing tariffs on Canadian steel, aluminium, lumber, automobiles, and other goods while Canada responds with counter-tariffs on American products. The article uses charts to show which provinces and states are most exposed, including Ontario, Quebec, and several US swing states.

The report also explains the broader business effect: some Canadian exporters are looking beyond the US market, while manufacturers deeply tied to cross-border supply chains remain vulnerable. Despite the pressure, Canada has seen strong foreign investment and GDP growth, making the trade fight a major test of strategy, resilience, and economic integration.

文章摘要

BBC News 報導，美國與加拿大的貿易爭端仍未解決。美國對加拿大鋼鐵、鋁、木材、汽車與其他商品加徵關稅，加拿大則以針對美國商品的反制關稅回應。文章用圖表說明哪些省份與州受影響最大，包括安大略、魁北克，以及幾個美國搖擺州。

報導也指出更廣泛的商業影響：部分加拿大出口商正在尋找美國以外的市場，但與跨境供應鏈高度連結的製造業仍然脆弱。儘管壓力存在，加拿大仍出現強勁外國投資與 GDP 成長，使這場貿易戰成為策略、韌性與經濟整合的重要考驗。

5 Daily Words

tariffs

taxes that a government charges on goods imported from another country.

In article: “Tensions have been simmering between the two neighbours since President Donald Trump returned to the White House just over 18 months ago, and unleashed a wide-ranging global programme of tariffs.”

關稅；政府對從其他國家進口的商品所徵收的稅。

levies

official charges or taxes collected by a government or authority.

In article: “Canada was one of the first countries the Trump administration hit with levies, and is one of two countries to respond with its own reciprocal measures.”

徵稅、徵收款項；由政府或主管機關正式收取的稅費或費用。

reciprocal

given, done, or felt by each side in return for what the other side does.

In article: “Canada was one of the first countries the Trump administration hit with levies, and is one of two countries to respond with its own reciprocal measures.”

相互的、對等的；一方做出行動後，另一方以相應方式回應。

retaliation

an action taken to punish or answer another action, especially in a dispute or conflict.

In article: “Canada has hit back with its own counter-tariffs on American goods, announcing on Tuesday what it calls a ‘dollar-for-dollar’ and ‘strategic’ retaliation designed to match the US tariffs.”

報復、反制；在爭端或衝突中，為回應或懲罰對方行動而採取的措施。

happenstance

chance or coincidence; a formal word for something that happens because of circumstances rather than deliberate planning.

In article: “Canada, by the happenstance of being neighbours with the world's largest economy, is hugely reliant on US trade.”

偶然、機緣；較正式的字，指某事因情勢或巧合而發生，而非刻意安排。
