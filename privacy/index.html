Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

Omarchy Journal Sync is a personal application used by its owner to synchronize journal-related information with the owner’s Google Calendar.

Google user data accessed

The app may request access to Google Calendar data needed to perform synchronization, such as calendar lists, calendar events, event times, event titles, descriptions, and related metadata.

How Google user data is used

Google Calendar data is used solely to provide the synchronization function requested by the owner: reading relevant calendar information, creating or updating journal-related calendar events, and keeping journal and calendar information consistent.

How data is stored

The app stores only the information needed to operate the synchronization, such as authorization tokens, synchronization state, and event identifiers. Stored data is used only for the owner’s personal synchronization workflow.

How data is shared

Google user data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties. The app does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, analytics resale, or any purpose unrelated to providing the synchronization.

Data protection

Access to the app and stored synchronization data is limited to the owner. Reasonable safeguards are used to protect credentials and synchronization data from unauthorized access.

Data retention and deletion

Synchronization data is retained only as long as needed to operate the personal sync workflow. The owner can revoke the app’s Google access at any time through their Google Account permissions and may delete locally stored app data.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the application owner.
